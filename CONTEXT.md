# Context Glossary

## Brand
Vibe From Cafe is an AI community for learning, sharing, and career growth through discussions, sessions, hands-on building, webinars, and podcasts, with an adjacent studio that helps businesses build useful AI.

## Domain
`vibefromcafe.id` is the public web domain for the Brand.

## Community Join Submission
A request from a person who wants to join the VFC community. It records identity, city, role, WhatsApp number, referral source, onboarding status, and timestamps.

## Project Inquiry
A lightweight business lead from someone who wants VFC to discuss or build an AI project. It is distinct from a Community Join Submission.

## Event
A public or draft community gathering. Events can be seeded from repository data and overridden or added at runtime through admin operations.

## Chapter
A local VFC community group organised around a place, such as Jogja.

## Deprecated Cafe Data
Archived cafe records copied from the upstream Vibe From Cafe repository. The data may support chapter context and stats, but cafe browsing belongs to `cafein.id`.
